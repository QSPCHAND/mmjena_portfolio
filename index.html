LIC's Jeevan Umang

Guaranteed 8% Annual Survival Benefit + Lump Sum

Ideal for creating a lifelong tax-free second income after a limited premium payment term (15, 20, 25, or 30 years), with life cover up to age 100.

  • 8% of Sum Assured paid every year post-PPT
  • Lump sum maturity + bonuses at age 100
  • Loan facility & Sec 80C / 10(10D) benefits
Flagship Endowment Age: 18 – 50 Yrs

LIC's New Jeevan Anand

Dual Benefit: Lump Sum Maturity + Lifelong Risk Cover

LIC's most trusted savings-cum-protection plan. Receive your full Sum Assured plus accumulated bonuses at maturity, while your life cover continues for life free of further premiums.

  • Guaranteed maturity payout for life goals
  • Free lifelong cover equal to Basic Sum Assured
  • Optional Accidental Death & Disability Rider
Children's Education & Marriage Parent Age: 18 – 50 Yrs

LIC's Jeevan Lakshya

Uninterrupted Child Goal Protection Plan

Designed specifically for parents to safeguard their child's higher education or marriage. Even in the parent's absence, future premiums are waived and annual income is paid until maturity.

  • Pay premiums for 3 years less than policy term
  • 10% annual income benefit if parent is not around
  • 110% of Sum Assured + bonuses at maturity
High-Cover Term Protection Age: 18 – 65 Yrs

LIC's Yuva Term / Jeevan Amar

Large Family Income Replacement at Low Cost

Protect your family's lifestyle and home loan liabilities with ₹50 Lakhs to ₹5 Crore+ pure life protection backed by LIC's sovereign trust and claim reliability.

  • Choice of Level or Increasing Sum Assured
  • Special discounted rates for women & non-smokers
  • Lump sum or monthly installment claim option
Guaranteed Lifetime Pension Age: 30 – 80 Yrs

LIC's New Jeevan Shanti

Lock Today's Interest Rate for Lifelong Pension

Single-premium deferred annuity plan that guarantees a fixed monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or annual pension for you and your spouse for life.

  • Guaranteed annuity rates locked at inception
  • Single Life & Joint Life (with spouse) options
  • Purchase price returned to nominee
Periodic Money Back + Bonus Age: 5 – 55 Yrs

LIC's Bima Ratna

Guaranteed Additions + Periodic Liquidity Payouts

Combines periodic survival payouts to meet recurring milestones with guaranteed yearly additions throughout the policy term for assured wealth growth.

  • Guaranteed Additions every policy year
  • Two survival payouts prior to final maturity
  • Short premium payment options (11, 16, 21 yrs)
